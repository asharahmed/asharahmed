### About

I am the founder and director of [Recourse Digital Services Inc.](https://getrecourse.ca), building self-help tools for consumers navigating federal regulatory processes. I lead the company's product, engineering, and security work.

I previously served in the federal public service with the [Canadian Digital Service](https://digital.canada.ca/), [Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/), the [National Research Council of Canada](https://nrc.canada.ca/), and [Employment and Social Development Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development.html), where I maintained infrastructure, data pipelines, and public-facing applications for government programs. At Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada I received the Director General's Award of Merit.

I received a Master of Science in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance from Western Governors University and a Bachelor of Computer Science from Dalhousie University. I am a Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP), with further certifications in security analysis and penetration testing.
